# 8. User Reactions

After each of them read their results I asked two things: was the thing I found planned or a
mistake, and which of the 3 points surprised them and would it change anything. Quotes are copied
from WhatsApp as they wrote them, Hinglish included, with an English line under each.

## Trader A

> "For me partial fill wala jyada serious hai. Slippage se profit kam hoga but agar hedge hi pura
> nahi laga then risk hi alag ho gaya. Real money increase karne se pehle definitely ye check
> karunga ki all legs expected qty se match kar rahe hai."

(For me the partial fill is more serious. Slippage cuts profit, but if the hedge wasn't fully
placed, the risk itself is different. Before increasing real money I'll definitely check that all
legs match the expected qty.)

Before that, about the NIFTY legs: "5:1 planned nahi tha. Long legs bhi 5 lots hi hona tha. ...
Honestly maine notice bhi nahi kiya tha. I was just checking overall pnl in kite." And about the
Rs 22,884 gap: "I know paper me fills better hote hai but itna difference hai wo nahi pata tha."

- When: 14 Sep 2026, 16:27 - 16:34, WhatsApp
- Screenshot: `05-contact-artifacts/user1-reaction-20260914-1634.png`
- What it changes: a leg-by-leg qty check (expected lots vs filled lots, flagged when it happens)
  goes above slippage and above P&L. He said it himself - "P&L se bhi important".
- One thing I'm not counting: at 16:31 I asked him "would that be useful?" and he said "Yes
  100%". That was a leading question, so I left it out. The quote above came before it.

## Trader B

> "Before changing limit/market order etc I need to know paper and live same instrument and same
> timestamp compare bhi kar rahe hai ya nahi. If comparison itself wrong then slippage number ka
> koi meaning nahi hai."

(Before changing limit/market orders I need to know if paper and live are even being compared on
the same instrument at the same time. If the comparison itself is wrong, the slippage number means
nothing.)

On the futures: "But futures me 130 points no way I will consider that normal execution cost."
On the options: "Options ka 0.5-1% honestly mujhe utna surprise nahi karta. I already assume some
slippage there." And if the comparison turns out to be right: "strategy live deploy karne layak hi
nahi hai at that size/time. Order type or entry logic change karna padega."

- When: 14 Sep 2026, 16:10 - 16:11, WhatsApp
- Screenshot: `05-contact-artifacts/user2-reaction-20260914-1611.png`
- What it changes: before any slippage number, first check that paper and live are the same
  instrument at the same time. Options slippage drops down the list since he already expects it.

Both of them, without me leading them there, put the same thing first: check that the two sides
genuinly match before looking at the money.
